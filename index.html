The road back
starts together

EuroShield Overland takes soldiers and veterans on overland expeditions — real terrain, real teamwork, and the kind of connection that helps people find their footing after service.

Apply for a trip Support the mission
01

What is needed

Many soldiers and veterans come home carrying injuries, stress and isolation. What helps most is often simple: time outdoors, a team around you, and a purpose to work towards.

Read more →

02

What we provide

Fully supported overland trips — vehicles, equipment, routes, food and an experienced crew — plus a community that stays in touch long after the trip ends.

How you can help

Donate, sponsor a participant, lend equipment or skills, buy our merch, or become a member. Every contribution puts another veteran on the road.

Our mission

Connection. Outdoors. Resilience.

We believe that the skills soldiers learned in service — teamwork, navigation, problem-solving under pressure — are exactly what overlanding asks for. On our trips, those skills become a bridge back to everyday life.

How to apply

For soldiers and veterans who'd like to join one of our trips.

  1. Who can apply

    Soldiers, veterans and their family members. No verification is needed at this stage.

  2. Fill in the short form

    Just the basics — we'll collect everything else later, in person or by phone.

    Are you a soldier, veteran, or a family member of one?

  3. We get in touch

    Our board reviews every application and contacts you by email, messaging app or phone.

  4. Get ready to roll

    We send you a kit list, the itinerary and everything else you need. We cover [travel / food / equipment].
